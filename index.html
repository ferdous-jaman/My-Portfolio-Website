About Me

Bridging Software Engineering & Artificial Intelligence

Passionate engineer combining rigorous software design with cutting-edge medical machine learning research.

I am a Computer Science & Engineering graduate based in Mirpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a proven track record across professional web engineering and peer-reviewed academic AI research.

At ElyXen, I build and maintain responsive, high-traffic, SEO-optimized web systems utilizing PHP, Laravel, WordPress, and modern JavaScript architectures. I care deeply about performance, clean code, database normalization (3NF), and robust software testing lifecycles (STLC).

Beyond full-stack development, my research focuses on Explainable Deep Learning, Medical Image Analysis, and handling extreme class imbalance in clinical datasets. My work has appeared in Informatics in Medicine Unlocked (Elsevier Scopus Q1, CiteScore: 10.5) and five prestigious IEEE international conferences.
